> This note includes a spoiler.

I watched Jobless Reincarnation season 3 episode 6 twice.

This episode was mostly between Rudy and Sara.
Sara, and how he parted ways with her, was one issue Rudy hadn't solved until this episode.
So the main focus in this episode was to solve it, and it started with Sylphie's kid and Rudy.
Honestly, I didn't recognize Suzanne at first, but once I did, it made sense that she had quit being an adventurer and gotten married.
And she even got a job as a nanny at Rudy's house.
Then, obviously, Rudy met Sara on the next job, but he avoided her, or ran away from her.
I was a little pissed off. Why would he do that?
But then he talked with her about the past and what happened, and showed how mature he had become.
In terms of Sara, she felt a little sad after talking to Rudy, and I thought she had regrets.
She might have, but after talking to Rudy she understood why it couldn't have worked, and she moved on. And then she and Rudy really became good friends.
I liked her.

Other than that, Roxy was so welcomed by the party and so respected.
I felt it was off that Rudy didn't do much during the fight and just stayed still, but I guess he let his guard down because Roxy was there.

Princess Ariel was a little involved and did one thing for Rudy, and more for Sylphie.
That one thing was how considerate she was.
Sylphie was like a worrier, and she accepted too much.
Or at least when I heard what she said, she sounded more like Rudy's mom than his wife.

From Eris's point of view, I didn't expect Nina to be able to use the Blade of Light.
But somehow, because of desire or resolve, Eris's Blade of Light was superior to Nina's.
Eris took a hit, but she was able to strike Nina's opening and blew her all the way into a wall.
Eris showed that she was putting her energy into her wooden sword, but at the same time, she was smiling softly by thinking about Rudy, and that was quite a good attitude.
It obviously showed how she had leeway while Nina rushed a little and lost.

Because Princess Ariel was a little involved, I felt like a new story would start.
But I don't know. Maybe... a war will come?


## Scores

- Episode: 7/10
- Rudeus: 7/10
    - I already forgot why he still had the hots for Eris
- Roxy: 8/10
    - She was our goddess.
- Sylphie: 6/10
    - Her dedication to Rudy was... too convenient for the story.
- Eris: 7/10
    - As always, she is stubborn. But she only cared about a few things.
- Nina: 6/10
    - Sounded like she had high hopes of reaching a higher position like the Sword King.
- Sara: 8/10
