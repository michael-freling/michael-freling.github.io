Episode 4 moves a lot of story points. Maybe one of the most important episodes since season 1.
I read a manga for this, but it's great how Ruby didn't feel anything about manipulating other people and talk with her smile and joy about how she behaved badly to others.
Also, on the anime, it's great expressions and voice performance to see how Aqua was disappointed and went to gloomy when he was all wrong. He was all emotional and even his alter ego blamed him in his mind. It's kinda pitty but this really gives me how he wants to live for himself.
And then, Akane. I didn't remember in the manga how Akane figured out Kamiki, but this episode on to that scene, too. Unlike Ruby's black star on her eyes, Akane has yellow star on her eyes and they also lightened up when she got a new actress award and she found out the Kamiki. It shows how Akane wants to help Aqua here as his girlfriend. That's a good comparison. Anyway, she should go into a police officer or FBI. 
Kana, on the other hand, also wants to help him as she was fallen in a love with him while he was just sitting on a bench but he made his head down. But Aqua rejected because he was in his own world at that time, and he was so shocked that he couldn't chase her nor he couldn't care her. For an half year to enjoy his own life with Akane really makes him peaceful, appearantly.
