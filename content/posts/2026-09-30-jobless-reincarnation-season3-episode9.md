> This note includes a spoiler.

I watched Jobless Reincarnation season 3 episode 9 twice.

So, this episode reminded me of the episode when Nanahoshi became hysterical when her summoning magic failed at once.
In this episode, because she thought she was going to die, she got hysterical again.
It was because she really missed her original world and wanted to do what she wanted to do.
She also didn't like that world because she hadn't aged at all.
This was something other series don't care about, but in reality, this should be a problem.
A person can easily die in an unfamiliar place from an unfamiliar virus or whatever, and that cannot be healed by magic.
That's actually a pretty good detail in the story.
It also rings true that empty words mean nothing to a person who is desperate.
By the way, Nanahoshi's drawings are more realistic, like her mouth or fingers, maybe to show she has a medical issue?

Why didn't Perugius allow Rudy to check the archive?
He said he didn't allow outsiders to go there, but is there any important book or something there?

The reason Perugius couldn't leave the fortress was that Laplace would come back.
When that happened and Laplace attacked humans with his demons, then he would need to stop him.
Because of that, he couldn't move and couldn't save Nanahoshi.
So, Rudy travelled to the Demon Continent to meet Kishirika, because she was alive 7,000 years ago and she might know about the illness of the first person who didn't have mana and be dead.
I wonder why humans got more mana 7,000 years ago and overcame the illness?

Anyhow, Rudy went to Rikarisu, which is a demon city.
I didn't expect that Nokopara, the horse man, had been a friend of Roxy's before and Elinalise met with him.
He somehow liked and trusted Roxy, so he wasn't going to trick any friend of hers.
Coming back to the Demon Continent and seeing these characters made me feel nostalgic.
And also, Kishirika hasn't changed a bit, but the animation was upgraded, so it was kinda... fun?
I don't know.

Laplace is gathering demons and will start a war against humans, and Rudy is going to the Demon Continent.
Is that some kind of sign that the war between demons and humans will start shortly?
I don't know.
In the next episode, I guess Rudy will meet the Demon King Artole and Kishirika, and maybe solve Nanahoshi's problem?
Let's see.
