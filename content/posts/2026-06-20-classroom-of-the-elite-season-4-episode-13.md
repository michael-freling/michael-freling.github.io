I watched the Classroom of the Elite, COTE, season 4, episode 13.
I finally liked Amasawa in Anime.
I didn't do in LN so much, but with watching animation for her, she is an emotional and mysterious girl, and that's nice.

Anyway, Amasawa came to Ayanokoji and Nanase's fighting place.
At first, Nananse didn't trust her at all because she saw someone with hair like Amasawa back when Kinoshita was hit by the cliff.
Well, it makes sense. Who can trust her?
Then she talked to Ayanokoji alone and revealed herself that she was from the White room.
And then she left.
Why did she come to him? I had no idea, but probably shouldn't think.
If I remember correctly, it was not that important.
Perhaps because she came near him and couldn't stop meeting with him and talking about herself.

Then, Ayanokoji left Nanase to Housen, and then he was trying to reach out to Sakayanagi while taking an exam.
He met Ibuki, who doesn't like the support from him, and also met Kiryuin.
Actually, Kiryuin's scene was hilarious.
It was a way better than I remembered, because an emotion less Ayanokoji was confused with how she reacted with his joke.
She is basically a Koenji in 3rd year grade as a woman.

Then he met with Shiranami or some sorts. I forgot who she was but she was the person in Ichinose's class and talked about the relationship between Ichinose and him.
It was very short conversation. Probably there was more in a LN, or maybe because there is talking in themselves that were not animated.
I think the important thing here is that they had a transceiver which he and Horikita discussed in this episode, and used it to talk with Sakayanagi.

The later part of an exam is about to begin, and it's going to be more interesting than the earlier part.
