> This note includes a spoiler.

I watched Jobless Reincarnation season 3 episode 10 twice.

When Kishirika tried to surprise everybody by saying there was no sokas grass, they were about to get mad but tried not to show it.
Their faces were so twisted, and it was funny.
And of course, Cliff was the first to say it while Rudy was trying to hold it back.
Then soldiers came and Moore spoke the human language instead of the demon tongue.
I thought he was a human, but it seems I was wrong.

They went to the Demon King's tower, which was a good one, a tower with a spiral staircase.
Rudy and Zanoba went to the throne to hand over Kishirika, while Cliff and Elinalise went to find the sokas grass.
When Rudy met the Immortal Demon King, Atofe, she turned out to be a selfish and arrogant king who never listened to anyone.
Kishirika actually warned them earlier so she was right.
All the soldiers, Rudy, and Zanoba tried to stop her and were blown away.
It looks like she was so strong, and she had lost only twice, to her husband Kalman, who was the North God, and to Laplace, so most of her soldiers were those people who couldn't flee from her.
How sad they were.
Anyhow, Moore allowed Rudy to leave with Kishirika.

But then Atofe was waiting for them in front of the warp gate.
So, Rudy and Elinalise planned how to fight her, and decided to let Cliff leave the place and ask Perugius to help them.
In the fight scene, and in this episode, Zanoba was so strong.
Was he this strong all along? I didn't remember but he was so tough that even Atofe couldn't move.
And it was interesting that the one Rudy couldn't stop wasn't Atofe, but Moore.
He said he was a master of magic so he recognized how skillful he was.
He used ice magic, quagmire, Disturb Magic, and even lightning, which even hurt his own side.
But it couldn't completely stop them, but he was enough to stop.

Then, Perugius came to the place and used his magic on Atofe because it was a good chance for him.
What happened to them? Was he angry with her or something?
He knew it wouldn't kill her completely and he didn't intend to break the pact, so he wasn't serious.
So, I didn't get what he was trying to do here.

Anyway, I feel like this fight can cause something bigger later.
The next episode will be "Turning Point 4", so something big will happen in the next episode, or at least the next few episodes.
