I watched Jobless Reincarnation season 3 episode 13 twice.

This episode was all about what happened to the future Rudy who showed up in the previous episode.
In the previous episode, there were no flashback scenes, just the two Rudies talking to each other, but in this episode, we were able to see what actually happened to the future Rudy.
This was depressing and heartbreaking.

So, as expected, first Roxy got the petrification after a few cats or rats had been seen.
And Cliff was the first to figure it out, and he also found out how and where to cure it.
So Rudy, Cliff, and Zanoba went to the Millis church to get the sacred text.
I thought they would get the god-class detoxification magic the official way, but they stole it!
That was something unexpected.
And of course they were chased, and on the way, Cliff got hurt and died.
In the last episode, I wondered when he had died, but he died even before Roxy did.
And he belonged to the Millis religion, but he even stole its bible to prioritize saving Roxy.
I liked Cliff even more here, but I got sad when he died.
Of course, Elinalise got mad at Rudy and hit him, but he was well aware that she had the right to do it.
Anyway, Roxy was already dead when Rudy went back to his home.

Without someone's help, he couldn't do it at all.
