First, the Nanase finally lured Ayanokoji into a place where nobody is there and started fighting against him.

But fighting scene was a good that Ayanokoji is as expectedly overwhelming her.
I still don't feel this scene and the motivation of Nanase doesn't really make sense, but she is 15 or 16 so make sense that she wants to put someone for her anger.

The another scene is, behind the scene of Ayanokoji and Nanase, Kushida was trying to take a picture of them, so they can expel him.
She was asked by Yagami, so he showed he outsmarted her completely.
However, Amasawa found her and obviously she couldn't deal with Amasawa at all.
Her physique is, as usual, extraordinary, and showed, what was that? Judo? so Amasawa even throw her.
With this physique and her obsession to Ayanokoji, which seems even crazy, it's so obvious that she is from the White room, but for readers, they don't know why she didn't try him to get expelled.

Ichinose is treated like one of the main heroine here instead of Horikita.
Well, I know what's going to come, so I wouldn't say that much.

This episode has a little action and also reveal a little truth so it was a good one.
