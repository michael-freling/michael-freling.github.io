> I am a light novel reader for this series

I watched the Classroom of the Elite, COTE, season 4, episode 4.
This episode was one of the episodes I was waiting for in this season the most.
Turned out that it was a little bit too fast after Ayanokoji found out what was Housen up to.
I expected Horikita was more like in a panic when he got a knife in his left hand and also Sudo was almost shocked, and also they needed to take care of its bleeding.
These entire reactions were removed and I felt they were too calm down even though they were just high school students in that school, except maybe Housen only.
His reaction was good and maybe a director or producer thought that was enough?
Also, I remembered he would say that he couldn't understand how Ayanokoji was so good before stabbing his knife, and that was one phrase I liked.

Besides, the exam showed that Ayanokoji's math score was 100 and better than anyone else, but there was no explanation how good it was, so the episode skipped a lot I felt.

Housen's fighting with Sudo and Horikita was kinda good, especially Horikita can actually fight and she could even do jump spinning kick.
They still couldn't even have a chance against Housen, though.
In this season, there will be more fighting scenes so maybe they'll be interested in.
