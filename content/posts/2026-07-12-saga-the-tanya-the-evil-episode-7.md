I watched the Saga of Tanya the Evil season 1 episode 7.
I watched this series a long time ago, and I liked it, but I forgot most of the story, so before the season 2, I decided to watch again.

This episode was no comedy but drama.
At first, the story began from the point of Major in the Entente alliance.
He said goodbye to his daughter, Mary, and wife because they left a city, and he got a christmas gift from Mary.
That was a submachine gun, and he wore it.

Then, the PoV was changed to Tanya.
She was trying to disagree with a plan of her high commander, who was trying to put full force into the Norden line despite her concerns of exhaustion and supplies.
She even showed her disatisfaction on her behavior like pouring milk to a coffee dirtily, and spilled over it on her desk.
Then she talked 2 other high ranks, one of them was Rerugen, and talked about the benefits of full force to the Norden line, and correctly guessed what the true purpose of this attack of the Empire was.
They planned to use that attack was a decoy and attacked from a rear as a surprise attack.
She was a logical employee in her previous life, so she was trying to find a rationale answer behind the intent of the tactices, but Rerugen at least couldn't believe she was able to figure out this plan by herself.
Because of this, or maybe planned from the beginning, 203rd battalion joined the campaign.

Tanya didn't like and she even had a back up plan in a case when she and first lieutenant would be dead for retretment.
She didn't emphasize even her own people, but she was logical to think these things too.
Then they started attacks to the rear of the Antent Alliance, the fjourd city or something.
Their plan was to destroy all canons in the port so battleships was able to come closer to the port, but the major of the Entente Alliance couldn't figure it out.
And he had them to destroy all of them.
Tanya even took a risk to fight one battalion by one company, but her company somehow managed to defeat them.
The major was actually the person who fought her in the Norden line in the 2nd episode, and he remembered her.
So he was trying to take her down, but right before she killed him off, her battalion destroyed all canons.
So instead of fighting, she decided to leave the city.
Well, it was a little bit dangerous for her not to attack that major in front of her, because he could attack her, wasn't it?
Maybe she is confident to protect his attacks completely.
Anyhow, Empire's battleships came, and the Entente Alliance couldn't stop them at all, so the battle was over, but the major didn't stop and tried to defeat Tanya.
Of course, he couldn't do it and Tanya killed him.
Then she found out his submachine gun was good, and decided to get it as her christmas gift.
That's the difference between a normal person who got a present from his daughter, with a wish that she wanted her father back, and the Tanya so-called Devil, who got whatever she thought it was useful for others.
It wasn't that cruel as it was a war, after all.

Then the top brass of the Empire was glad of the success of this fight.
But the Mary and her mother were crying in a place, who left from that city before it was attacked.
