> This note includes a spoiler.

I watched Jobless Reincarnation season 3 episode 11 twice.

Because Rudy had a nightmare about Atofe, I thought Atofe would become another trauma for Rudy, but it seems not.

Nanahoshi got completely better. She needs to drink tea made from sokas grass every day to keep her illness away, but anyway, she got better and she was really grateful to Rudy.
She had slapped him last time, but he forgave her anyway, and she even started showing him respect for the first time.
They got along even better, and she wondered what his previous life was like, but he didn't want to talk about it.
Well, he was nothing like the current Rudy back then, so it totally makes sense.

Then Cliff got a reward from Kishirika: a right eye like Rudy's, but its ability is to see all kinds of information about each person.
He didn't say what kinds of information, but can he see whether Rudy was reincarnated from another world?
I don't know.
Maybe that wasn't a big issue either way.

Then Perugius started to like Rudy.
He really hated demons, and the blow he dealt Atofe in the last episode was so good for him without him risking anything.
Because he was able to get a chance to do so thanks to Rudy and Zanoba, he came to like them.
I hadn't noticed that he didn't rely on or trust Rudy that much before, though.
Maybe because his magic seemed like Laplace's?
Zanoba also talked with Perugius a lot, and he also knew Ruijerd had saved Perugius before.
Rudy was impressed when Zanoba talked about it, and it seemed he had forgotten it, so he tried to bring Ruijerd up with Perugius.
Anyway, as a reward for dealing with Atofe, he got something from Perugius that he can use to call him.
And I thought Princess Ariel was just sitting there, but when she brought up that she could bring statues, Perugius wasn't really interested in it.
And instead, he asked her a question and she couldn't answer.
The question was "What is the quality of a king?"
What was that answer? I recalled something from Ling Yao from Fullmetal Alchemist, and it sounded similar to what Rudy said.

Anyhow, Rudy went back to his home, and hugged Roxy as he promised. Husband and wife moment.
Then he talked about what happened and then he wrote a diary, and then Man God came to his dream.
I knew because the title of this episode was "Turning Point 4."
Then he talked about what would've happened if he had followed what Man God said.
And everyone, including Roxy and his father, would've been saved.
This sounded terribly wrong.
Without him, Roxy would've been dead for sure in the dungeon.
There was no way Paul and the others could've defeated the hydra without her and Rudy.
So, they would probably all have died, or at least some of them would have.
Anyway, Man God asked Rudy for a favor this time, which sounded pretty small.
Because Rudy has a high EQ and felt sorry for Man God, he wanted to do what Man God said.
At the end, Man God showed a creepy, vile smile in the animation, but it could still be just a misleading scene.
Anyhow, Rudy was trying to go to his sanctuary in order to find something unusual, as Man God had told him.
But then, an old guy came into the room out of the blue and told Rudy that he was from the future, proving it by saying Rudy's name from his previous life.
I hadn't realized Rudy had never told anyone his name from his previous life, but now thinking about it, he only talked about the past life to Nanahoshi, and he didn't really want to talk about it, so there was no way anybody could know.
And old Rudy said he had been deceived by Man God, but that was where the episode ended.

I don't know what will happen if he enters the basement.
In the first place, what was the "something unusual"?
And also, who is Man God?
My guess is that Man God is actually Laplace, because that name has come up so many times so far.
And Man God was actually sealed like Perugius said, so he was trying to come back to life.
Also, that means just like he tricked Ruijerd's people, he was trying to trick Rudy.
I don't know what the "something unusual" was.
But maybe it could take over Rudy's body so that Man God could come back into the world.
Then probably starting a war or something?
Even if he wasn't Laplace, he sure was a legendary person like Orsted or Perugius and could cause a devastating result.

Anyway, what will the next episode be about?
Old Rudy will talk about what will happen in the future, and Rudy will choose not to do that.
But the title is "An End and a Beginning," so something big will start for sure.
